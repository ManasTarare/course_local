LOCALIZATION PROFILE

Who is this for?

AI adapt + translate uses Gemini to suggest course terms and localize idioms, then DeepL to translate with the generated glossary. Preview stays local. Gemini and DeepL receive course text when you choose translation.

✳

What gets translated?

Common fields like titles, descriptions, lesson text, quiz questions, answers, captions, and alt text.

What should you review?

AI mode can rewrite idioms and cultural text. Actual image files and embedded documents are not in a JSON upload, so their visuals remain unchanged.
